# INTRODUCTION
**Title:** Thor: Ragnarok
**Overview:** Thor: Ragnarok follows the God of Thunder as he tries to save Asgard from his power-hungry sister, Hela. After she destroys his hammer and banishes him to the planet Sakaar, Thor is forced to fight the Hulk in a gladiatorial arena. He ultimately escapes with Hulk, Loki, and Valkyrie, returning home to unleash the demon Surtur to destroy Asgard and defeat Hela for good.

**Genre:** Action, Fantasy
**Release Date:** November 03, 2017

# SYPNOSIS
**Summery:** After the events of Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015), and four years after the events of Thor: The Dark World (2013), Thor (Chris Hemsworth), held captive on the planet Sakaar without his hammer, must win a gladiatorial duel against The Incredible Hulk (Mark Ruffalo) and assemble a team (made up of The Incredible Hulk, Valkyrie (Tessa Thompson), Thor, and Loki (Tom Hiddleston)) to stop the villainous Hela (Cate Blanchett) and the impending Ragnarök, the doom of the Asgardian civilization.

# CHARACTERS
_• Thor Odinson (Chris Hemsworth):_ The Asgardian God of Thunder who must escape Sakaar and stop the apocalypse.
_• Bruce Banner / The Hulk (Mark Ruffalo):_ A brilliant scientist and Thor's fellow Avenger who has spent two years trapped inside his Hulk form on Sakaar.
_• Loki Odinson (Tom Hiddleston):_ Thor's adoptive brother, the God of Mischief, who has secretly been ruling Asgard disguised as Odin.
_• Valkyrie (Tessa Thompson):_ A former elite Asgardian warrior living as a scavenger and bounty hunter (Scrapper 142) on Sakaar.
_• Heimdall (Idris Elba):_ The former all-seeing guardian of the Bifröst who helps hide and protect surviving Asgardians.
